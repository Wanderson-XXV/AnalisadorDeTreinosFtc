5.1 — Backend: api/scouting.php

Endpoint POST /scouting.php — cria um scouting_round (recebe match_id, team_number, scout_id), marca is_locked=1 e locked_at=NOW, retorna o registro criado com scout_username.
Endpoint GET /scouting.php?id=X — retorna o scouting_round com seus scouting_cycles embutidos.
Endpoint PATCH /scouting.php?id=X — finaliza o scouting (end_time, total_duration, observations, robot_issues, strategy_notes, is_locked=0).
Endpoint DELETE /scouting.php?id=X — cancela/exclui o scouting_round e seus cycles.
Valida duplicata via UNIQUE(match_id, team_number) já existente — retorna 409 se já existe.
5.2 — Backend: api/scouting_cycles.php

POST — cria um scouting_cycle (mesmo schema do cycles.php mas vinculado a scouting_round_id).
PATCH?id=X — edita hits/misses/zone de um cycle existente.
